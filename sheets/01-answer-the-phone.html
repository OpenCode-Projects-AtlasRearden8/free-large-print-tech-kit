Answer the Phone

Who is calling, and what do I do?

  1. When it rings, press the big green circle (or swipe it up). Put the phone flat to your ear like a regular phone.
  2. Speaker button: look for the little icon of a person with sound waves. Tap it, and the voice comes out loud from the phone itself. Tap again to go back to private.
  3. Done talking? Press the big red circle. The call is over. Nothing bad happens if you press it.
  4. If you missed a call, the phone shows “Missed call” at the top. Tap it to see who called, then tap the name to call back.
  5. Voicemail: if you don't answer, the phone records a message. A dot or badge on the green phone icon means you have one. Tap the phone icon, tap Voicemail, and tap the message to listen.
  6. Unknown number? It is always OK to let it ring and not answer. A real person will leave a message. A scammer will not.
  7. Numbers you don't know that call twice in a row can still wait. You can return an important call — real emergencies reach you by the people you know.
Remember: answering and hanging up is safe. You cannot break anything by pressing the green or red circle.
