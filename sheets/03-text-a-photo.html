Text a Photo to Someone

Send a picture in four taps.

  1. First, take your picture if you haven't already. Tap the camera icon, aim, and press the big white circle shutter button at the bottom. Wait for the small click.
  2. Open Messages (the green speech bubble) and tap the name of the person you want to send it to. No conversation yet? Tap the new message button, type a few letters of their name, and tap it.
  3. Tap the little + sign or camera/paperclip icon near where you type. Your photos will appear from today going backward.
  4. Tap the photo you want. A check mark or blue dot appears on it. Tap Send (usually an arrow or speech bubble).
  5. How do you know it went? The message bubble moves up, and it shows the picture inside it. If it sits with a “Not delivered” note, tap Try again.
  6. To look at a photo you received: tap it once to make it fill the screen. Tap it again, or press the home bar at the bottom, to go back.
Remember: the person can see your photo within seconds, and you will see when they say thanks. It uses your normal phone plan or Wi-Fi — the data cost is tiny.
