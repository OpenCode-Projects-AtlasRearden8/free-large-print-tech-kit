Charge the Phone

Plug it in. That's the whole skill.

  1. Plug in every night at the same place — like your toothbrush. Pick one outlet (bedside is good) and always use it. Charging overnight is fine; the phone stops when full.
  2. Finding the port: the cable plugs into the bottom edge of the phone and fits one way. If it resists, don't force it — flip it over.
  3. Push the cable in firmly, until it stops. Most “my phone won't charge” problems are just a cable that is not pushed all the way in.
  4. You'll know it is charging when a lightning bolt or bar appears near the battery icon and the percentage starts creeping up.
  5. Battery icon is red? It is below about 20%. Plug it in soon wherever you are — it's not an emergency.
  6. Keep it out of heat. No hot cars or sunny windowsills. If the phone feels hot, unplug it and move it somewhere cool.
  7. Still not charging? Try a different outlet, then check with a family member — worn cables are cheap to replace.
Remember: you cannot over-charge a modern phone. Leaving it plugged in overnight is normal and safe — and you never start the day low.
