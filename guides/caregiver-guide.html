Caregiver Guide

Which sheet to laminate — and how to hand it over well.

This kit is six one-page cheatsheets for a relative who is new to their smartphone or nervous about it. Your job takes twenty minutes: print, laminate the right two or three, tape them where they will be seen, and keep the rest in a folder.

Which sheets to laminate

If your relative…Laminate these
misses calls or can't end or reroute a call1 Answer the Phone
wants to see family but never initiates2 Video Call the Grandkids
gets photos but never sends any back3 Text a Photo
gets texts, calls, or emails from strangers4 How to Spot a Scam
is often at 5% or “forgot to charge it”5 Charge the Phone
clicked something odd or follows pop-ups6 Safe Habits on the Web

Default starter kit: laminate sheets 4 (Scam) and 5 (Charge) for everyone. Scam protection is the highest-stakes sheet; charging is the highest-frequency problem. Add the matching sheet for their biggest ask — usually 1 (calls) or 2 (video).

Where to put them

  • By the phone (bedside or kitchen chair), at eye level when seated, never in a drawer.
  • By the charger outlet: sheet 5, taped to the wall. Same outlet every night is half the battle.
  • In a folder for later: sheets that don't apply yet, ready to trade in when needs change.

Handover tips

  1. Walk through one page together, once. Then stop teaching — the sheet does the rest.
  2. Let them lead. “Which sheets should we laminate?” goes further than “here's everything.”
  3. Revisit monthly. Swap the old sheet for the next one when the current one stops getting read.
  4. Praise use, not mastery. Every ignored scam call is a win.

Printing

Print on Letter paper at 100% scale (“Actual size”, not “Fit to page”). Each sheet is exactly one page. Color and black-and-white both stay readable. Laminate 5-mil pouches, or use a taped-down sheet protector — the glossy surface wipes clean.

Privacy: this kit collects nothing and never touches the internet. Read the sheets together and don't fill them in; write on the laminate in dry-erase instead.
